Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data.

1. Data Collection
Our app does not collect or store any personal information. Any audio recorded using the app remains solely on your device and is not uploaded, shared, or stored on external servers.

2. Data Usage
The app only processes your voice locally to provide playback functionality. Your recordings are never transmitted outside of your device.

3. Data Sharing
We do not share your data with any third parties.

4. Data Security
As no data is collected or stored, there is no risk of unauthorized access to your data from our side.

5. Your Rights
Since the app does not collect or store data, you have full control over any recordings on your device. You can delete them at any time.
